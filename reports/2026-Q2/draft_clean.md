- **Long-Only Scope**: Form 13F filings reflect exclusively long US equity positions and exclude short positions, cash holdings, foreign exchange, commodities, and unlisted securities.
- **45-Day Reporting Lag**: Filings report quarter-end positions and are submitted up to 45 calendar days after period close, meaning observed positions may have been modified prior to publication.

- **Hedge-Only Signals May Reflect Hedging**: Signals driven exclusively by hedge funds may represent arbitrage legs, statistical arbitrage baskets, or delta hedges against short positions rather than unhedged directional conviction.
- **Small Discretionary Sample**: The discretionary cohort comprises 3 institutional allocators (Berkshire Hathaway, CalPERS, Temasek Holdings); consensus across 2 funds represents a small sample size that warrants qualitative verification.
- **Estimated Dollars Added**: Dollars added figures are estimated as shares added multiplied by quarter-end closing price; actual purchase prices vary across the quarter.
